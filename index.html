I am a probabilist. I am currently a Reader and Royal Society University Research Fellow at the University of Bath, in the probability group Prob-L@B. From 2018 to 2021, I was a Prize Fellow at the University of Bath. From 2016 to 2018, I was the G. H. Hardy Junior Research Fellow at New College and the Mathematical Institute at the University of Oxford. From 2013 to 2016, I was a PhD student in the Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford. My supervisor was Alison Etheridge.

I am interested in stochastic processes motivated by biology, reaction-diffusion equations, coalescent processes, branching processes and interacting particle systems.

In 2024, I was awarded the Erlang Prize by the INFORMS Applied Probability Society. I currently serve as an Associate Editor at Annals of Applied Probability.
